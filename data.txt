Barney's River | 45.56235420669231, -62.22581256315235 | Barney’s River Nature Reserve was newly designated in 2022. It can be accessed via Weaver’s Mountain Road, a well-maintained gravel road. The forest exhibits many of the characteristic signs of a hardwood old-growth forest in Nova Scotia’s eastern mainland: a full and high canopy, pits and mounds from decades of succession, and a lush fern understory.
